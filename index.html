Feature Added

1. Seamless Light & Dark Mode System

A complete dual-theme engine has been implemented across the website with smooth CSS variable transitions and local storage persistence:

  • ☀️ Light Mode: Crisp `#f8fafc` canvas, pure white elevated cards with soft diffusion shadows, deep slate `#0f172a` headings, and sapphire blue `#2563eb` action buttons.

  • 💾 Auto-Saving Preference: User choice is stored in localStorage, with automatic fallback to system OS preference via window.matchMedia('(prefers-color-scheme: ...)').

Critical Defect

1. Inventory Gap & Missing Brands (TikTok Inventory Disconnect)

The project was supplied with a real-world inventory sheet (Laptops on TIKTOK.txt) containing 69 verified models, yet the website currently only lists 7 models:

  • ⚠️ Total Absence of Lenovo: Lenovo is the single largest inventory brand on TikTok (13+ high-demand units including ThinkPad T14 Gen 1, X1 Yoga, X13 Gen 4, T480s, X1 Extreme Gen 2, Yoga 6, L13, T490s), yet Lenovo does not even exist in the catalog or brand filter!
  • ⚠️ Missing Gaming / Discrete GPUs: The user has models like Dell Alienware M15 R7 (Ryzen 7 6800H) and Dell Precision 5560 (Core i9), but the store displays zero gaming laptops. All 7 laptops have integrated graphics.
  • ⚠️ Broken Recommendation Match: If a buyer selects "Gaming" or "Dedicated GPU" in consult.html, the algorithm fails to find any exact match because all 7 laptops have gpu: 'integrated'.

Functional Flaw

3. Mock E-Commerce & Missing Cart Drawer

The cart and purchasing mechanisms are currently non-functional placeholders:

  • ⚠️ Fake Cart: Clicking + Add to Cart merely increments an integer counter (cartCount++). Items are never recorded into state or localStorage.
  • ⚠️ No Checkout or Drawer: Clicking the Cart button in the navbar triggers openCartToast() which just displays "You currently have X item(s) in your cart." There is no order summary, quantity controls, shipping form, or COD order placement.
  • ⚠️ Stub Handlers: The "Sign In" button only calls a toast notification, and email subscription does not submit or validate data.
  • ⚠️ Dead Links: All footer legal and policy links are empty href="#" anchors that jump back to top.
Performance Defect

4. Oversized Assets & Visual Background Inconsistencies

Assets are directly causing layout clashes and slow loading times:

  • ⚡ Massive JPEGs: macbook-pro-m2.jpg (1.71MB) and hp-spectre-x360.jpg (1.80MB) total over 3.5MB for just two photos, severely hurting Largest Contentful Paint (LCP).
  • ⚡ Clashing Photo Backgrounds: While the HP and Dell units have clean transparent PNG cutouts, the MacBook, Spectre, and Surface use opaque real-photo backgrounds. In Light Mode, these render as mismatched dark rectangular photo boxes inside bright white cards.
Technical Bug

5. Cross-Origin `file://` Iframe Security Error

When running as local files (e.g. directly opened in Chrome via file:///):

  • 🛡️ Chrome blocks direct JavaScript DOM access (storeFrame.contentWindow.applyTheme()) under the Same-Origin Policy (origin null accessing null). This silently fails and prevents the portal theme switcher from updating the inner store.
  • 🛡️ volts.html lacks Light Mode support entirely, causing a stark contrast between a white portal header and a pitch-black inner page.
UX & Accessibility

6. Cursor & Interaction Issues

  • 🖱️ body { cursor: none; } breaks native operating system accessibility cursor tracking, lags on high-refresh screens, and disappears when hovering near frame borders.
  • 📞 WhatsApp links use the placeholder phone number +92 300 123 4567.
  • 📅 Copyright notices and badges are dated to 2025 instead of 2026.
Design Comparison

7. Comparison of the Two Concepts

⚡ VOLTS (Minimalist Cyber-Tech)

Aesthetic: Ultra-dark `#080808` with vibrant neon chartreuse `#e8ff00` accent.

Typography: `Syne` (bold futuristic geometric display) paired with `DM Sans`.

Audience: Global developer & creator audience in USD.

🇵🇰 LaptopHUB (Refined E-Commerce)

Aesthetic: Modern dual-mode obsidian / soft-slate with royal electric blue accent.

Typography: `Playfair Display` paired with `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

Audience: Pakistani corporate & student market in PKR with COD and local warranty.
